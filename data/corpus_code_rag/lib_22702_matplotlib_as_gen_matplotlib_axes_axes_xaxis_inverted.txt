# matplotlib._as_gen.matplotlib.axes.axes.xaxis_inverted

matplotlib.axes.Axes.xaxis_inverted   Axes.xaxis_inverted()[source]
 
Return whether the xaxis is oriented in the "inverse" direction. The "normal" direction is increasing to the right for the x-axis and to the top for the y-axis; the "inverse" direction is increasing to the left for the x-axis and to the bottom for the y-axis.
