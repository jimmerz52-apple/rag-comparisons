bands  
List of all bands of the source, as GDALBand instances. >>> rst = GDALRaster({"width": 1, "height": 2, 'srid': 4326,
...                   "bands": [{"data": [0, 1]}, {"data": [2, 3]}]})
>>> len(rst.bands)
2
>>> rst.bands[1].data()
array([[ 2.,  3.]], dtype=float32)
