transform(X) [source]
 
Transform X. This is implemented by linking the points X into the graph of geodesic distances of the training data. First the n_neighbors nearest neighbors of X are found in the training data, and from these the shortest geodesic distances from each point in X to each point in the training data are computed in order to construct the kernel. The embedding of X is the projection of this kernel onto the embedding vectors of the training set.  Parameters 
 
Xarray-like, shape (n_queries, n_features) 

If neighbors_algorithm=’precomputed’, X is assumed to be a distance matrix or a sparse graph of shape (n_queries, n_samples_fit).    Returns 
 
X_newarray-like, shape (n_queries, n_components)
