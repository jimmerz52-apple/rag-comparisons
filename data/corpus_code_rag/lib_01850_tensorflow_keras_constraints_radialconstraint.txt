For example, the desired output for the following 4-by-4 kernel: kernel = [[v_00, v_01, v_02, v_03],
          [v_10, v_11, v_12, v_13],
          [v_20, v_21, v_22, v_23],
          [v_30, v_31, v_32, v_33]]
 is this:: kernel = [[v_11, v_11, v_11, v_11],
          [v_11, v_33, v_33, v_11],

          [v_11, v_11, v_11, v_11]]
 This constraint can be applied to any Conv2D layer version, including Conv2DTranspose and SeparableConv2D, and with either "channels_last" or "channels_first" data format. The method assumes the weight tensor is of shape (rows, cols, input_depth, output_depth). Methods get_config View source
