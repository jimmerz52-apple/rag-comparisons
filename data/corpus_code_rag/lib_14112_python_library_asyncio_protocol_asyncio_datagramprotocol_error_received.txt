# python.library.asyncio-protocol#asyncio.DatagramProtocol.error_received

DatagramProtocol.error_received(exc)  
Called when a previous send or receive operation raises an OSError. exc is the OSError instance. This method is called in rare conditions, when the transport (e.g. UDP) detects that a datagram could not be delivered to its recipient. In many conditions though, undeliverable datagrams will be silently dropped.
